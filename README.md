FixIt SA

FixIt SA is a South African tradespeople marketplace that connects customers with local service providers.

Services

- Electrical work
- Plumbing
- Gas installations
- Building and renovations
- Painting and general maintenance

Purpose

To help homeowners, landlords and businesses find tradespeople and help artisans connect with potential customers.

Project status

Demo prototype. Online bookings, payments and user accounts are not yet fully operational.
